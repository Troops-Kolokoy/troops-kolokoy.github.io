How it trades

It waits for gold's busiest hours, then acts.

Gold makes most of its moves when the big markets open. JS Gold Session watches six moments inside those sessions and does nothing in between.

The six trading windows sit inside these sessions. Their exact times and settings stay inside the robot.

Reads first

It reads the hour before it trades

When a window's key hour closes, the robot checks the candle and decides whether to follow the move or trade against it.

Protected

Stop loss on every trade

The stop is placed the moment a trade opens and sized to how fast gold is moving at that time.

Lets winners run

No fixed take profit

Once a trade is in profit, a trailing stop moves up behind price and locks in the gain.

Your risk

Fixed lot or risk %

Choose a fixed lot or a percentage of your balance per trade, with a maximum-lot cap for extra safety.

On your chart

A live dashboard

Equity, today's P/L, win rate, open trades and history, plus Pause, Close All and Breakeven buttons.

Set and forget

Automatic broker time

It finds your broker's clock by itself, so the windows always land at the right hour on your Exness account.

FAQ

Questions people ask before they start.

Do I need trading experience?

No. The robot finds the entries, sets the stop loss and manages the exit. You only choose your lot size or risk %.

Which broker do I need?

An Exness account on MetaTrader 5, opened through our Exness partner link. The free month is for accounts opened through that link.

How much do I need to start?

We recommend at least $200, so the robot can keep each trade's risk small.

Does it trade every day?

It checks six windows every trading day and only trades when its rules are met. Some days it takes several trades, some days none.

What happens during a losing streak?

Each loss is capped by its stop loss. The robot never doubles the lot or adds trades to win it back, so a bad run stays a controlled run.

Is my money safe?

Your money stays in your own broker account and we can't withdraw it. Every trade has a stop loss, but trading always carries risk and losses do happen.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Yes. MT5 must be running for the robot to trade. Many traders use a VPS so it runs all week.

Can I run it on more than one account?

One licence covers one MT5 account. Message us for extra licences.
